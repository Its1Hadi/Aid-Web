How to request help

THREE SIMPLE STEPS

Requesting help with Aid

Share details that help explain your situation and location.

1

Choose a status

Select critical, urgent, or stable to describe the type of assistance you are requesting. These labels organize the demo and are not a medical diagnosis.

2

Describe your location

Enter a nearby landmark, such as a gate, building, clinic tent, or walkway, so a helper can understand where you are.

3

Add useful details

Briefly describe what is happening and include relevant information from your health profile if needed.

STATUS OPTIONS

Choose the option that best describes your request

Critical · حرج

For a critical emergency request.

Urgent · عاجل

For a request needing prompt attention.

Stable · مستقر

For a non-urgent request or inquiry.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or nearby official medical staff directly.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin

Does Aid send a real emergency alert?

No. This course prototype simulates requests and responses. It is not connected to dispatchers or medical teams.

How can I describe my location?

Use a recognizable nearby landmark, such as a gate, building, clinic tent, or walkway. The prototype does not send GPS coordinates.

Should I enter real medical information?

No. Use fictional details while testing. The prototype does not provide secure medical storage.
